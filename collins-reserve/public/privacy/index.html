Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026 · Applies to PostPilot (https://collins-reserve-demo-42b6.web.app)

PostPilot is a content publishing dashboard that lets you queue posts, publish them to your TikTok account, and prepare them for WhatsApp Status. This policy explains what we store and why.

What we collect

  • Content you create — titles, captions, hashtags, media files, and scheduling times you submit through the dashboard or API. Stored in our database to show your queue and perform the posts you request.
  • TikTok authorization data — after you connect TikTok, we store an access token, refresh token, your TikTok open ID, and display name, solely to publish content on your behalf and to show connection status. Tokens are stored server-side, never in the browser.
  • Dashboard API key — stored only in your browser's localStorage to authenticate you with the backend. It is never transmitted anywhere except our own API.
  • Usage logs — basic server logs (time, endpoint, status) for debugging failed posts.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • We do not read your TikTok direct messages, followers, or private activity — only the basic profile info needed to confirm the connection, and the posts you explicitly request.
  • We do not use your content for advertising.

Third parties

  • TikTok — processed under TikTok's Partner Privacy Policy when you publish.
  • WhatsApp — sharing to WhatsApp Status uses your device's native share sheet; content passes through your device, not our servers.
  • Firebase (Google) — hosting, database, and file storage.

Retention & deletion

Content remains until you delete it from the dashboard. You can disconnect TikTok at any time from Settings, which revokes and deletes stored TikTok tokens. You may request full deletion of your data by contacting us.
